Which size should I order?

Small fits up to 1 year, Medium 1–2 years and Large 2–3 years. Between sizes? Go one up. Still unsure? WhatsApp us your baby's age and weight, or see our size guide.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery — you pay the rider when your parcel arrives. No card needed.

Yes, we deliver all over Pakistan. Delivery time and charges are confirmed on WhatsApp after you order.

Yes. Message us within 3 days of delivery and we'll arrange an exchange for unworn items with tags. See delivery & exchange.
