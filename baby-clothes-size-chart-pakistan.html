Age or height — which one to trust?

Age is only a starting point. Two babies of the same age can be a full size apart, so height matters more than age. If your baby is tall for their age, choose by height. If they are chubby, check the weight column too.

How to measure your baby at home

  • Height: lay your baby flat on a bed, mark the top of the head and the heel, then measure the distance with a measuring tape.
  • Weight: use the weight from your last check-up, or weigh yourself with and without the baby and subtract.
  • Compare with a dress that fits: measure an outfit that fits well now and send us the length — the easiest way to be sure.

5 tips before you order baby clothes online

  • Between two sizes? Go one size up — babies grow fast and a slightly loose suit still looks cute.
  • For winter clothes, leave room for a vest or inner underneath.
  • Dungarees with adjustable straps last longer because you can loosen them as your baby grows.
  • Buying a gift? Pick the size for the age the baby will be in 2–3 months.
  • Always confirm the size on WhatsApp before cash on delivery — it avoids returns.

Questions parents ask

What size is a 1 year old baby in Pakistan?

Most 1 year old babies fit Medium (12–18 months). If your baby is tall or above 10 kg, Medium is right; if small for their age, Small (6–12 months) may fit better.

What size should I buy for a 6 month old baby?

Small (6–12 months) fits most 6 month old babies and leaves room to grow for a few months.

Should I buy one size bigger for babies?

If your baby is between two sizes, yes — choose the bigger size. For winter clothes, a little extra room is also useful for an inner layer.
