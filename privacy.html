Privacy policy

We collect only what we need to deliver your order: your name, mobile number, city, address and any notes you add at checkout or in our contact form.

How we use it

  • To confirm and deliver your order, and to contact you about it on WhatsApp or by phone.
  • To answer messages you send us.

We never sell your information. We share your delivery details only with the courier delivering your parcel.

Cookies and advertising

We use the Meta Pixel to understand which of our Facebook and Instagram ads bring visitors to the shop. It records actions such as viewing a product, adding to bag and placing an order, and uses cookies to do so. It does not receive your address. You can control ad personalisation in your Facebook and Instagram ad settings.

Your bag and wishlist

Your bag and wishlist are saved in your own browser on this device. They are not sent to us until you place an order.
