Size guide

We stock three sizes: Small, Medium and Large. Tell us your baby's age and weight and we'll confirm the right one before we ship.

SizeFit
SmallOur smallest size, for younger babies
MediumOne step up, room to crawl and play
LargeOur biggest size, for older babies and toddlers

How to measure

  • Height: lay your baby flat and measure from the top of the head to the heel.
  • Weight: use your most recent check-up weight.
  • Between two sizes? Go one size up — babies grow fast.

Still not sure? Send us your baby's age and weight on WhatsApp and we'll recommend a size.
