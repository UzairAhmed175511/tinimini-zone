This chart is for all Tinimini ZONE products — dungaree sets, 2-piece sets, fleece tracksuits, winter suits and denim suits. Not sure? WhatsApp us your child's age and height and we'll confirm the size.

How to measure

  • Height: lay your baby flat and measure from the top of the head to the heel.
  • Weight: use your most recent check-up weight.
  • Between two sizes? Go one size up — babies grow fast.
