Baby Winter Clothes Guide for Pakistan (Winter 2026)

Sardi aa rahi hai! Here is a simple guide to dressing your baby warm without overdoing it — what to buy, which fabrics work, and how winters differ across Pakistan.

By Tinimini ZONE · Updated 19 October 2026

The simple layering rule

A useful rule many parents follow: dress your baby in one more layer than you are wearing. Layers are better than one very thick outfit, because you can take one off when you go indoors or the sun comes out.

  • Inner layer: a soft cotton vest or body suit next to the skin.
  • Middle layer: a full-sleeve shirt, sweatshirt or fleece dungaree.
  • Outer layer: a jacket or warm suit for going out, plus socks and a cap.

Check warmth by feeling the back of your baby's neck or tummy — it should feel warm, not sweaty. Hands and feet often feel a little cool even when the baby is comfortable. Avoid heavy layers and caps while your baby sleeps indoors, and ask your doctor if you are unsure.

Best fabrics for baby winter clothes

  • Fleece: light, soft and warm — great for dungarees and tracksuits.
  • Cotton: best for the layer next to the skin because it breathes.
  • Denim with a warm inner: stylish for family visits and outings on milder days.

Winter in different cities of Pakistan

  • Karachi: winter is mild and short. A full-sleeve 2-piece set or light fleece is usually enough during the day; add a layer at night.
  • Lahore, Faisalabad, Multan: foggy, cold mornings and nights. Fleece dungarees or tracksuits with an inner layer work well.
  • Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Peshawar: colder evenings — plan a warm suit, socks and a cap for going out.
  • Quetta and the north: very cold winters; add a heavy jacket over the winter suit.

Baby winter clothes checklist

  • 4–5 cotton inners or body suits
  • 2–3 fleece dungarees or tracksuits for daily wear
  • 1–2 smart winter suits for weddings, birthdays and family visits
  • Warm socks, a soft cap and mittens for newborns
  • A blanket or wrap for the car and evenings out

Questions parents ask

How many layers should a baby wear in winter?

A common rule is one more layer than an adult would wear in the same place. Use a cotton inner, a warm middle layer like fleece, and a jacket for going out.

Is fleece good for babies?

Yes, fleece is soft, light and warm, which makes it a popular choice for baby winter dungarees and tracksuits. Put a cotton layer next to the skin.

What winter clothes does a baby need in Karachi?

Karachi winters are mild, so full-sleeve 2-piece sets or light fleece are usually enough in the day, with one extra layer at night.
