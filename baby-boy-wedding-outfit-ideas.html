Baby Boy Wedding Outfit Ideas for Shaadi Season

Shaadi season is here! Ideas to make your little one look smart at mehndi, baraat and walima — while keeping him comfortable through a long evening.

By Tinimini ZONE · Updated 19 October 2026

Outfit ideas by function

  • Mehndi: bright and fun. A colourful kurta, or a light shirt with a waistcoat, matches the mood and is easy to move in.
  • Baraat: the dressiest event. A smart 3-piece suit or a vest set with a full-sleeve shirt gives the "little groom" look.
  • Walima: elegant and calm. Soft colours like white, beige, olive or navy look great in photos.
  • Dholki and family dinners: a denim set or 2-piece set — smart but relaxed.

Winter wedding tips

  • Wedding halls can be warm while outdoor events get cold after sunset — dress in layers you can remove.
  • A full-sleeve shirt under a vest or suit adds warmth without hiding the outfit.
  • Carry a warm jacket or blanket for the drive home.

Comfort comes first

  • Soft fabrics near the skin — avoid scratchy embellishment around the neck.
  • Choose outfits that make diaper changes easy.
  • Pack a spare outfit; little ones and wedding food don't always mix!
  • Try the outfit at home a few days before so you can fix the size.
