Delivery & exchange

Delivery

  • We deliver all over Pakistan.
  • Payment is cash on delivery — pay the rider when your parcel arrives.
  • After you order, we confirm your size, address, delivery charges and expected delivery time on WhatsApp.
  • Orders are dispatched once confirmed. Delivery usually takes a few working days depending on your city.

Size exchange

  • Message us on WhatsApp within 3 days of receiving your order.
  • Items must be unworn and unwashed, with original tags and packaging.
  • Exchanges are subject to stock availability. Delivery charges for exchanges may apply.

Damaged or wrong item

If something arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, send us a photo on WhatsApp within 48 hours and we'll make it right.
