Our story

Tinimini ZONE started with a simple wish: baby clothes that look adorable in photos and still feel soft at bedtime.

We pick styles we'd dress our own little ones in — easy to put on, comfortable to crawl and play in, and sweet enough for family visits and special days. Every order is packed with care and sent across Pakistan with cash on delivery, so you only pay once it's in your hands.

We're a small team, and we answer every message ourselves. If you're unsure about a size or want to see a colour in natural light, just ask on WhatsApp.
